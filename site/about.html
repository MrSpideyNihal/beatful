About Beatful

Bringing classic traditional card games into the modern digital era.

Beatful is a modern, high-performance digital recreation of the beloved traditional card game Sevens (popularly known as Satte Pe Satta in India and 7 of Hearts internationally).

Our Mission

Our goal is simple: to create the most fluid, visually stunning, and fair multiplayer card gaming experience accessible to anyone, anywhere—whether on a smartphone app or straight inside a web browser.

Key Principles

  • Zero Pay-To-Win: Beatful is built around pure skill, card counting, and tactical strategy. Every player has equal odds.
  • Instant Connectivity: Play online over mobile data or WiFi with zero setup, or challenge smart AI bots offline.
  • Universal Platform: Native Android release builds and full web browser compatibility powered by WebAssembly & Flutter.
  • Vibrant Design: Rich glassmorphism aesthetics, dynamic card flight animations, responsive landscape/portrait layouts, and celebratory win effects.

Game Rules Overview

The game uses a standard 52-card deck. The player holding the 7 of Hearts (7♥) opens the match. Players take turns laying down cards connected sequentially to the 7s (6s going down to Ace, 8s going up to King). If a player has no legal moves, they must pass. The first player to empty their hand wins with 0 points, while other players incur penalty points according to remaining card pip values (Ace = 1, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13).
