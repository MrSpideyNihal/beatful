Game Rules

How To Play Beatful (Sevens)

1

The Opener

The player holding the 7 of Diamonds (7♦) opens the first round by playing it to the table.

2

Connecting Suits

Players lay down connected cards: 7s open new suits, 6..A go down, 8..K go up.

3

Pass & Win

If you have no playable cards, pass your turn. The first player to empty their hand wins!
